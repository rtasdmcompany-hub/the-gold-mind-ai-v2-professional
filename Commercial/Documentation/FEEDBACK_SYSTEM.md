# FEEDBACK_SYSTEM.md

**Phase 8 · Sprint 8**  
**Goal:** Structured listening that improves product without chaos  
**Rule:** Feedback never auto-changes Core Trading behavior

---

## 1. Feedback channels

| Channel | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Feature Voting | Prioritize roadmap transparently |
| Bug Feedback | Supplement tickets with severity vote / “still happens” |
| Satisfaction Rating | CSAT after ticket resolve / wizard complete |
| Product Review | Opt-in public/testimonial pipeline (moderated) |
| Improvement Suggestions | Free-text themes → Product |
| Commercial Survey | Periodic NPS / renewal drivers |

---

## 2. Feature Voting

```
Submit idea → Deduplicate → Publish to board (optional)
  → Customers vote → Product reviews top N per quarter
  → Status: Under review · Planned · Shipped · Declined
```

Declines include brief reason (scope / freeze / Market compliance).

---

## 3. Bug Feedback

- Link to ticket id when possible  
- Capture: version · edition · frequency  
- Hot bugs escalate via Support Operations P1/P2  

---

## 4. Satisfaction Rating

| Moment | Ask |
|--------|-----|
| Ticket closed | 1–5 CSAT + optional comment |
| Wizard finished | “Was activation clear?” |
| After update | Optional one-click |

Target: short — never a 20-question wall mid-trade.

---

## 5. Product Review

- Invite after 14+ days active license  
- Moderation for claims compliance  
- No incentivized fake reviews  

---

## 6. Improvement Suggestions

- Single textarea + category tag  
- Route to Product inbox weekly digest  

---

## 7. Commercial Survey

| Cadence | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| Quarterly | NPS · renew intent · support feel |
| Post-churn (cancel) | Why left · win-back eligible? |

---

## 8. Privacy

- Aggregate where possible  
- Don’t attach private diagnostics to public boards  
- Honor opt-out  

---
