## 1. Welcome Email

**Subject:** Welcome to THE GOLD MIND PROFESSIONAL

## 2. License Delivery

**Subject:** Your license key — THE GOLD MIND  

Body: Masked/display key · Portal “My Licenses” · Activation Guide · Device limit note · Do not share key  

---

## 3. Activation Success

**Subject:** Activation successful  

Body: Device name · seats used/max · Open Dashboard · Welcome Wizard CTA · Diagnostics tip  

---

## 4. Subscription Renewal Reminder

**Subject:** Renewal reminder — {plan} · {date}  

Variants: T-30 / T-14 / T-7 / T-1  
Body: Plan · price · renew CTA · manage subscription · what happens if expired (grace)  

---

## 5. Update Available

**Subject:** Update available — version {x.y.z}  

Body: Highlights (≤5) · Release Notes link · Backup reminder · Update / Market path by edition · Rollback note  

---

## 6. Maintenance Notice

**Subject:** Scheduled maintenance — {window}  

Body: What is affected (portal/license/update — not customer’s broker) · Start/end UTC · Status page link  

---

## 7. Support Ticket Confirmation

**Subject:** Ticket {id} received — {category}  

Body: Priority · first-response expectation · KB suggestions · how to add diagnostics · don’t send passwords  

---

## 8. Password Reset

**Subject:** Reset your THE GOLD MIND portal password  

Body: One-time link · expiry · ignore if not you · security tip

## Template variables (common)

`{customer_name}` `{edition}` `{license_masked}` `{version}` `{ticket_id}` `{plan}` `{renew_date}` `{device_name}` `{support_url}` `{portal_url}`

## Anti-patterns

- No “guaranteed returns”  
- No raw full license keys in plain subject lines  
- No urgency dark patterns on renewal beyond factual dates
