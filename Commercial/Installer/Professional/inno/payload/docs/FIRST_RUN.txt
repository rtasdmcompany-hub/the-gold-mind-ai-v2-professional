THE GOLD MIND PROFESSIONAL — First Run

1. Open THE GOLD MIND PROFESSIONAL from the Start Menu.
2. Click Activate License and enter your portal email + license key.
3. Click Deploy EA to MetaTrader 5.
4. Open official MetaTrader 5 (MetaQuotes).
5. In Navigator open The Gold Mind Professional and attach TheGoldMindAI_Professional to a chart.
6. Load preset Phase18_DEFAULT_PROTECTIONS (range 250, activations=1 — no re-arm).
7. Enable AutoTrading.

Power config lock: H4 range max 250 pips · max 1 activation per level per H4.
