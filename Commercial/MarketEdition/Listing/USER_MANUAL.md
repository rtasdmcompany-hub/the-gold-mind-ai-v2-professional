# USER_MANUAL.md

**Edition:** THE GOLD MIND MARKET · Version 2.0.0  

## Overview

THE GOLD MIND MARKET runs as an Expert Advisor on MetaTrader 5. It uses the certified Core Trading Engine and manages only trades tagged with its configured Magic Number.

## Inputs (groups)

Refer to the EA Inputs dialog. Typical groups in the certified build include Identity/Ownership, Dashboard, AI Core, Risk, Session, Validation, and Production settings. Do not change trading behaviour by editing source — Market customers receive the compiled Market build.

## Operation

1. Attach EA to chart.  
2. Confirm AutoTrading is allowed.  
3. Monitor Journal for runtime messages.  
4. Use Strategy Tester for historical study before live capital.  

## Support

Use MQL5 Market product comments and THE GOLD MIND Market support policy. Website Customer Portal is a separate product.
