# INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md

**Edition:** THE GOLD MIND MARKET  

1. Open MetaTrader 5.  
2. Open **Market** tab → search **THE GOLD MIND MARKET**.  
3. Purchase / download via MQL5 Market (official channel only).  
4. Restart MT5 if prompted.  
5. Open **Navigator → Expert Advisors** and locate the product.  
6. Attach to the desired chart.  
7. Enable **Algo Trading** / AutoTrading as required by your terminal.  
8. Review **Inputs** (Magic Number, risk, dashboard) then OK.  

**Do not** install Website Professional `.exe` packages for Market use.  
**Do not** enter Website portal license keys — Market licensing is handled by MetaQuotes.
