# GLOBAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md

**Product:** THE GOLD MIND AI

**Owner approval required before each gate**

Use this as a go / no-go board.  
Unchecked items = not launch-ready.

## Gate A — Product identity

- [ ] One-sentence product definition approved  
- [ ] Primary persona approved (e.g., serious XAUUSD systematic trader)  
- [ ] Secondary persona approved (investor / desk lead — if any)  
- [ ] Explicit non-goals published (what Gold Mind is *not*)  
- [ ] Edition matrix approved (Website / Market / Internal)

---

- [ ] Risk disclosure (trading loss warning)
